StarSlip Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Short version: StarSlip does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What StarSlip stores

StarSlip saves a few settings only on your own device, using your browser's local storage:

  • Your best scores (Endless and today's Daily Challenge)
  • Which comet skin you picked
  • Whether sound and vibration are on or off

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes it.

What StarSlip does not do

  • No sign-in or account
  • No collection of names, emails, contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties

Sharing your score

If you tap Share score, your phone's own share menu opens with a short message and a link. You choose whether and where to send it. StarSlip does not see or keep what you share.

Services the app uses

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your device downloads the game files. Their own privacy policies apply: GitHub, Google.

Children

StarSlip does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/Brandon-ECE/starslip/issues.
